Cancellation and Refund Policy

The ₹49 Host Pass is a prepaid digital service activated after verified payment.

Effective 23 August 2026

The Host Pass does not auto-renew, so there is no recurring mandate to cancel. You simply do not purchase another pass.

Non-refundable after activation

Because digital hosting access becomes available immediately after payment verification, a successfully activated Host Pass is normally non-refundable, including where it was unused, partially used, or the customer changes their mind.

When we will review a refund

We will review a request for a duplicate charge, an amount charged without subscription activation, a confirmed technical failure that prevented access throughout the paid period, an unauthorised transaction reported promptly, or where a refund is required by applicable law.

How to request review

Email replace-before-live@example.com with the host email, Razorpay payment ID, payment date and reason. Submit the request within seven days of payment when reasonably possible. Do not send card, bank-account, UPI PIN or OTP details.

Processing

If approved, the refund is sent to the original payment method. Bank and payment-provider processing times apply. Deleting an account does not itself create a refund.

Legal rights

This policy does not limit non-waivable consumer rights or Razorpay dispute rights available under applicable law.
