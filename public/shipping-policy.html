Shipping and Delivery Policy

PlayVerse sells only digital access. No physical product is packed or shipped.

Effective 23 August 2026

Digital delivery

After Razorpay confirms a matching captured payment, the 30-day Host Pass is added to the signed-in account automatically. Activation is normally immediate, but delayed bank confirmation or webhook delivery may take additional time.

No shipping charge

There are no courier, postage or delivery charges. The total displayed checkout price is for digital access only.

Delayed activation

If payment was deducted but access is not active, do not pay again immediately. Sign in again and allow the payment status to refresh. If it remains inactive, contact replace-before-live@example.com with the payment ID.

Service area

Digital access is provided online where PlayVerse and Razorpay are lawfully available. Users are responsible for internet and compatible-device costs.
