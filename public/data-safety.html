APP DISCLOSURE

Data Safety Summary

This page helps users and app reviewers understand PlayVerse data handling. The full Privacy Policy controls.

Data associated with a host account

  • Email address and display name for account management.
  • Purchase history identifiers and subscription status for payment verification.
  • App activity such as room creation and temporary game state for core functionality.
  • Diagnostics, device/browser and security logs for reliability, fraud prevention and security.

Guest data

Guests provide a temporary display name and room code. No guest account is required. Temporary session and connection identifiers are used to run and reconnect the room.

Microphone

Microphone access is optional and used only for live voice after permission. PlayVerse does not intentionally collect or store the audio. It is transmitted to other participants using WebRTC.

Payment provider

Razorpay may collect contact, payment and device/security information needed to process a transaction. PlayVerse stores payment references, not full payment credentials.

Security and deletion

Data is encrypted in transit over HTTPS, passwords are hashed, and hosts can request account deletion online. Some transaction records may be retained where legally required.

The Play Console Data Safety form must be updated to match the exact Android build, SDK versions and production providers used at release time.
