Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Quartz Web Solutions handles information when you use PlayVerse.

Effective 23 August 2026

1. Information we collect

  • Host account: email address, display name, encrypted password hash, account dates and subscription expiry.
  • Payments: Razorpay order ID, payment ID, amount, currency, verification status and time. We do not store full card, bank-account or UPI credentials.
  • Game session: room code, display name, game state, connection identifier and a temporary reconnection token.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser/device information, timestamps, security events and server logs that hosting or security systems generate.

2. Voice and microphone

Microphone access is optional and starts only after you allow it. Voice is sent in real time to other room participants using WebRTC. PlayVerse does not intentionally record or store the audio. Connection metadata may pass through signalling and network-relay providers when technically necessary.

3. Why we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts, verify subscriptions, operate private rooms, reconnect players, prevent fraud and abuse, troubleshoot failures, answer support requests and comply with legal obligations.

4. Service providers

Information may be processed by Razorpay for payments, our hosting/database providers for service delivery, and network providers for real-time connections. Each provider processes data under its own terms and applicable law. We do not sell personal information.

5. Cookies and local storage

Hosts receive a secure, HTTP-only session cookie. The browser stores a temporary room reconnection token, install preferences and a pending order reference. These are necessary for sign-in, reconnection and payment status recovery.

6. Retention

Temporary rooms are removed when they end or the server restarts. Account and verified transaction records are retained while the account is active and as needed for tax, fraud, dispute and legal obligations. Security logs are retained only as long as operationally necessary.

7. Security

We use password hashing, secure cookies, rate limits, restrictive browser security headers, server-side payment signatures and amount/status checks. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Your choices and rights

You may decline microphone permission, leave a room, sign out or delete your host account. You may request access, correction or other rights available under applicable law by contacting us. Some transaction records may be retained when legally required even after account deletion.

9. Children

PlayVerse is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly create host accounts for children. Contact us if you believe a child supplied personal information.

10. Contact
